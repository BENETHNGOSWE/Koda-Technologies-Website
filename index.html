Image Based POS

An Image-Based POS displays product images on screen, making it easy for cashiers to identify products while invoicing.

Online/Offline POS

Offline GST billing solution ensures seamless transactions, even without an internet. It automatically syncing data to the cloud when reconnected.

V-Pay

When a retailer initiates UPI transaction, a QR code instantly appears on the hardware device, allowing customers to scan and pay effortlessly.

OCR

It simplifies supplier bill processing by extracting product details from uploaded invoices. This eliminates manual stock entry.

Material Management

Using material management features, retailers can remove expired or damaged products from inventory and ensure accurate stock updates supported by warehouse software.

Centralized Multilocation Management

Discount & Offer
