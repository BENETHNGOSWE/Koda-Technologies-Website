Our Story

From everyday challenges to practical digital solutions.

Koda Technologies was co-founded in 2022 by a team of developers dedicated to providing smart ICT solutions to businesses and individuals.

We help businesses break free from repetitive manual work—data entry, inventory tracking, invoice generation and report compilation—so teams can focus on growth.

Our work includes custom software development, ERP configuration and system integration, including TRA integration with Odoo and Frappe.

Our clients include freight forwarding, manufacturing, retail and growing SMEs across East Africa. We build collaboratively, using regular feedback to keep solutions aligned with real workflows.
